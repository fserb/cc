cc: a design pass

What I would change after reading the apple-design and emil-design-eng skills against cc as it is today (screenshots of nadya.gum.ts.net/cc at 1280x800). Before is a copy of today's CSS; after is the proposal. The after frame works: click the tool buttons, press rows, hover a reply, type in the box. The table at the end lists each change and the rule behind it.

Left out on purpose: the list's motion when sessions start, end or move stays as it is (user); no animation on list navigation or anything opened by a key (Emil: used tens of times a day); no springs or drag gestures, since cc has nothing you drag. Not mocked, worth asking: a dark scheme (Apple's "colours that adapt to light and dark"); cc has none, and it would touch every colour in index.html. The phone keeps its pop-up under the tool buttons with the dimmed page, which you already chose.
