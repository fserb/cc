pending bubble: cancel and send-now buttons

Each variant shows a sent message, then a pending one ("sending…"), a stale one (no ack after 2 min) and a failed one. ✕ takes it back into the box; ↑ sends it now. The buttons stay at full opacity while the bubble fades. Every button has a 44px touch area, larger than what it draws. The "hold to take back" notes are gone.
